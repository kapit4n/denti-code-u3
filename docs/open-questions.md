| 17  | May an appointment be booked with a dentist or chair that is **not active**? | **Answered: no.** `createAppointment` and `rescheduleAppointment` refuse an inactive clinician or chair with `UNBOOKABLE_RESOURCE` (409 `DOMAIN_RULE_VIOLATION`), and the message names the resource. `onlyActive` still defaults to _no filter_ on the two list endpoints — a clinician who has left stays visible on the appointments they worked. | Reschedule of an appointment whose clinician was deactivated _afterwards_ is refused too, so the booking has to be reassigned. The clinic accepted that. See [ADR 0020](./decisions/0020-booking-use-case-declares-its-rule-repositories.md). | **Closed**          |

| 18 | What happens when a clinician re-opens a closed visit? | **Partly answered.** `reopenVisit` puts the visit back to `OPEN` and clears `ended_at` (ADR 0022). The linked **appointment is not touched** — it stays `IN_TREATMENT` until the front desk completes it through `POST /api/v1/appointments/:id/status`, and reopening does not re-open a completed booking. | **Nothing records that a reopening happened.** `updated_at` moves, but there is no actor, no `reopened_at`, no history: an amendment and an ordinary edit look identical. The audit-log requirements (question 15) are what would answer it, and they arrive with authentication in Milestone 12. Until then a re-opening is silent, which is the honest cost of building it now rather than the whole trail. | Milestone 12 |

| 19 | May the **same patient hold two open visits at once**? A patient cannot literally be in two chairs, and the rule "refuse a second open visit for the same patient" may well be right. | **Assumed: no rule yet — two open visits are currently allowed.** It applies to _both_ doors (`startVisit` and the walk-in), and the appointment door shipped without it, so adding it to the walk-in alone (ADR 0024) would make the two doors disagree. `findOpenForPatient` already exists in the repository, written and waiting for this rule or its caller. | The first clinic to ask for it gets both doors changed at once, in one slice, with the existing repository method doing the reading. Implementing it now on one door would ship a configurable disagreement instead. | The visit workspace (Milestone 6), where an open visit is visible and the question becomes real. |

**Rule:** do not build any of these speculatively. Resolve them at the milestone
where they become blocking, and write an ADR if the answer changes an
architectural decision.
